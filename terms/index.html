Terms & Privacy

Last updated: November 2026 · Headline is made for Sacred Heart CMI Public School.

1. Terms of use

  • Who can use it. Headline is a learning tool for students and staff. Use it kindly and for school-appropriate purposes.
  • Your account. Keep your password and recovery code private. You are responsible for activity on your account. Admins may suspend accounts that misuse the service.
  • AI-generated content. Briefings are written by AI using web search. They can contain mistakes or outdated information. Please check important facts with a trusted source and use Report an error when something looks wrong.
  • Fair use. Daily limits apply to protect the service. Do not try to break, overload or bypass it.
  • Sharing. If you create a share link, anyone with the link can read that briefing until you stop sharing it.
  • Availability. The service is provided as is, may change, and may be paused for maintenance.

2. Privacy policy

What we store. Your username, a password that is handled and hashed by Firebase Authentication, your saved briefings (archive), favourites, tags, quiz results, XP, streak and badges, your chosen interests, and simple usage records (the date, your username and counts of briefings and quizzes). We do not ask for your email, phone number or real name.

Why. To sign you in, keep your archive, run the leaderboard, enforce daily limits and see how the app is used so it can be improved.

Cookies and local storage. Essential storage keeps you signed in and remembers settings such as theme, language and reduce-motion. With your consent we also use Firebase (Google) Analytics to count visits and feature use in aggregate. You can choose Essential only and change your mind any time from Cookie settings in the footer.

Service providers. Google Firebase (accounts, database, hosting of data, analytics if you accept) and Google Gemini (and, if enabled, Anthropic Claude) to write briefings. The topic, language and date you choose are sent to these services to produce the briefing. Please do not type personal information into the focus box.

Who can see your data. Your archive is private to you and to school admins who manage the service. Your username, level and XP may appear on the leaderboard unless you hide yourself in your profile.

Keeping and deleting data. Data stays while your account exists. Ask an admin to delete your account and archive at any time.

Children. Headline is intended for school use under the supervision of the school. Parents or guardians can contact the school to ask about or remove a child's data.

3. Contact

Questions or requests: please speak to your teacher or the Headline admin at Sacred Heart CMI Public School.
