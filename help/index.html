Help & FAQ

Quick answers for students and teachers.

What is Headline?

Headline writes a short news briefing for you in English, Malayalam or Hindi, with top stories, sports, a quiz and a listen-aloud mode.

Why can't I generate another briefing?

Each account gets a set number of briefings per day (usually one). When you've used it, the button shows your saved briefing and when the next one unlocks (midnight). Admins can change the limit.

Why is the language fixed?

The language follows the day: Saturday, Sunday and Monday are English; Tuesday and Wednesday are Malayalam; Thursday and Friday are Hindi. The labels in the app can be switched any time from the language menu.

How do XP, levels and streaks work?

You earn XP for briefings and quizzes. Levels rise as XP grows. Take at least one quiz a day to keep your streak. After 5 pm you'll see a reminder if your streak is at risk.

I forgot my password

Choose Forgot password? on the sign-in box and enter your recovery code (shown when you signed up). No code? Ask an admin to reset it, then make a new code in My account.

How do I share a briefing?

Open a briefing and press Share. You get a link, plus WhatsApp, X and Telegram buttons and a print-friendly page. Press Stop sharing to disable the link.

Can I install it like an app?

Yes. Use Install app in the menu, or on iPhone tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Is the news always correct?

It's written by AI using web search, so mistakes can happen. Use Report an error on a briefing and check important facts with a trusted source.
